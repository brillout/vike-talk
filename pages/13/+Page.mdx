## Open Source Pricing

- 100% MIT licensed
- 100% free for engineers
  - No license need — works like a normal open source tool.
- Only larger companies pay
  - They see a pesky toaster in dev: `Purchase a license at ...`
    - Vike heuristic detects whether app is developed by a team
  - Pay only how much you can/want

**It's not a bug, it's a feature.**
